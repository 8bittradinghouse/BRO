BRO OUTCOME TRUTH DOCTRINE

Classification

- `current owner`
- observational-only interpretation layer

Purpose

Outcome truth measures what happened after decisions and execution.
It must never rewrite runtime authority or make the system look better than it
was.

Core rules

- runtime truth flows into outcome truth
- outcome truth must not reinterpret runtime rows into a second dialect
- profitability is not proof of correctness
- loss is not proof of incorrectness
- outcome truth does not modify runtime behavior

Outcome-only concepts

- `claim_boundary_class`
- `record_claim_boundary_class`
- `outcome_truth_status`
- `execution_realism_class`

These may classify completeness or realism.
They may not rewrite lifecycle posture, blocker ownership, or lane permission.

Retired live vocabulary

Outcome truth must not treat stage-family or old runtime-posture aliases as
current-owner runtime language. If historical artifacts still contain that
older vocabulary, it must stay explicitly historical or replay-only.
