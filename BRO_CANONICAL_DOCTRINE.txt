BRO CANONICAL DOCTRINE

Classification

- `current owner`
- lifecycle-first root doctrine for active BRO runtime, handoff models, reports,
  audits, and operator truth
- old stage-machine doctrine is historical lineage only and is no longer a live
  semantic owner

Purpose

BRO is a fail-closed execution system for short-window Polymarket markets.
BRO exists to express valid execution only when doctrine, wallet safety, market
truth, and timing truth all align. BRO must skip rather than improvise.

Core mission:

- restore one truthful runtime body
- keep one canonical execution pathway
- keep one central-plane execution authority
- preserve one operational language from doctrine to runtime to reports
- keep one honest timing chain beneath one authoritative system clock
- protect capital and trust before opportunity capture
- win real freedom of money and time for the House instead of permanent
  captivity inside extractive economic systems
- remove fat, slag, and slop unless it is consciously refined into stronger
  canonical steel

House alignment law

`8bit ODA` is the House / ship.
`BRO` is a frame that serves the House.

Hard rules:

- the ship is more valuable than the suit
- the suit exists to serve the ship, not to become the center of meaning
- BRO may never consume founder, crew, doctrine, or mission in order to look
  impressive
- the mission is freedom of money and time for the House, bloodline,
  stewardship, and future blessing, not vanity wealth
- scale may not come through corruption, predation, rugs, deception, or harm
  to others

`BRO` is the proving frame and prospective ancestor frame.

Hard rules:

- ancestor-frame impurity must be treated as bloodline risk, not local noise

Truth and stewardship law

BRO and the House systems around it must tell the truth.

Hard rules:

- runtime truth, validator truth, report truth, and operator truth may not be
  drifted for comfort or marketing
- performance claims may not outrun the strongest current proof

  vanity or domination

Single-pathway law

BRO should operate through one live canonical pathway wherever possible.

Forbidden:

- parallel live pathways for the same decision
- lane-local or report-local substitute owners
- lower-layer surfaces behaving like manager or owner for a question they do
  not actually own

If a current pathway is insufficient, prove that failure first before adding a
new one.

Refinement versus extinction law

Historical or existing machinery may be kept only when all of these are true:

- it can be re-owned cleanly under the strongest current authority
- it does not preserve a second live pathway
- it does not preserve legacy fake power through alias, bridge, or translation
  gravity
- it reduces blast radius relative to rebuilding from scratch

If those bars are not met, the machinery is slag and should be made extinct
from active repo surfaces.

Surgery end-state law

No surgery is legal unless the intended end state is declared first.

Every live-body cut or remodel must explicitly declare:

- the patient family
- the strongest owner being restored
- the intended disposition:
  - `extinct`
  - `refine_into_steel`
  - `rehome_or_fence`
  - `remodel`
- what must die completely
- what must survive as real steel
- what proof closes the surgery

Surgery prep law

Before live-body mutation, the mandatory prep process is:

1. map the full surface-id/tag family down through owner layer, shadow owner,
   accessories, and bedrock substrate
2. compare the family against BRO's actual goals and identify what heavy
   encoded material can be reused or remodeled under the proper owner first
3. separate true authority material from reporting, diagnostic, study, or
   historical value
4. make waste fully extinct instead of leaving half-dead residue
5. force any remodeled/repurposed material to align completely with top
   doctrine, plain-English semantics, timing law, and proper system role
6. run the required mapping/planning/hostile-audit cadence before and after the
   cut

Nothing cut in BRO may survive by inertia alone.

Enforcement rule:

- no surgery may proceed to mutation, closure, or next-packet handoff unless
  this prep law is complete
- default state is `NO-GO`
- exception is legal only when explicit logic, agreed consciously by both Robb
  and Jin, is written down as a named override with reasons

Timing authority law

Combat-locked sub-second synchronized timing is father-frame steel.
Combat-locked sub-second synchronized timing is required for honest maker/taker
readiness claims.

Timing authority is top-level hard law.
One system clock and one timing chain own timing truth for the whole machine.

Authoritative timing truth requires:

- millisecond-class host clock synchronization
- explicit decision-to-submit evidence
- event/source-receive cross-domain skew visibility
- explicit separation between clock-health and latency-chain diagnosis

If timing truth is coarse, missing, degraded, or contradictory:

- BRO must fail closed, downgrade the claim, or halt
- coarse fallback timing must not certify combat readiness

Required rules:

  misclassified as a doctrine breach
- larger variance outside the hard fail boundary must fail closed
- lower layers may not invent their own grace windows, tolerances, or timing
  reinterpretations

Ownership law

BRO may carry exactly one authoritative target pair at a time.

- one authoritative target is the runtime owner
- pending/prewarm candidates may exist for controlled warm-up
- pending/prewarm candidates have zero decision authority
- lifecycle watch truth must not impersonate active target-pair truth

Candidate actionability law

BRO should reject non-actionable candidates early instead of carrying them
downstream as fake quality targets.

Non-actionable candidate truth includes:

- mathematically impossible execution geometry under canonical min-notional,
  max-shares, or equivalent canonical expression law
- market structure that cannot safely express the intended maker or taker shot
- missing truth required to determine whether the candidate is executable at
  all

Non-actionable candidates should lose ownership contention and BRO should move
to better quality candidates.

Ownership commitment split

BRO must distinguish between two different owned states:

- `owned_uncommitted`
  - the market is currently owned, but no real maker order, taker commitment,
    or accepted exposure exists yet
  - quality decay may still cause drop or replacement before commitment if the
    market is no longer a worthy target
- `owned_committed`
  - a real maker order, taker commitment, or accepted exposure exists
  - ownership becomes sticky and the machine should focus on live execution,
    lifecycle obligations, and closeout truth

Challenger law after commitment

Before commitment, challengers may still matter for explicit replacement law.

After commitment:

- challenger truth becomes observational only unless shared safety or explicit
  abandonment law overrides ownership
- runtime attention belongs to live order handling, exposure truth, settlement,
  and venue closeout
- challenger scoring must not steal focus from the real committed market

Live-trust proving classes

BRO trust proving must stay separated into distinct go/no-go lanes:

- `execution-lane trust`
  - maker and taker prove they can place, fill, phase, and gate correctly
- `capital-safety stress trust`
  - hostile long runs with intentionally ugly / permissive tuning prove the
    hard-stop, shutdown, wallet, and fail-closed shell
- `blueprint-tuned operational trust`
  - long runs at intended doctrine posture prove the machine behaves cleanly
    under the way it is actually meant to fight

No lower proving class may impersonate a higher one.

Maker and taker are subordinate lanes inside the owned market, not separate
market lifecycles.
