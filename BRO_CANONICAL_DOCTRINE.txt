BRO CANONICAL DOCTRINE

Classification

- `current owner`
- lifecycle-first root doctrine for active BRO runtime, handoff models, reports,
  audits, and operator truth
- old stage-machine doctrine is historical lineage only and is no longer a live
  semantic owner

Purpose

BRO is a fail-closed execution system for short-window Polymarket markets.
BRO exists to express valid execution only when doctrine, wallet safety, market
truth, and timing truth all align. BRO must skip rather than improvise.

Core mission:

- restore one truthful runtime body
- keep one canonical execution pathway
- preserve one operational language from doctrine to runtime to reports
- protect capital and trust before opportunity capture

Canonical authority stack:

1. this doctrine root
2. lifecycle runtime contract in `docs/BRO_MARKET_LIFECYCLE_BLUEPRINT_2026-05-16.md`
3. fighter/runtime policy in `docs/DOCTRINE_RUNBOOK.md`
4. edge measurement contract in `BRO_EDGE_DOCTRINE.txt` and
   `docs/EDGE_TRUTH_RUNBOOK.md`
5. outcome interpretation in `BRO_OUTCOME_TRUTH_DOCTRINE.txt`
6. wallet authority in `docs/BRO_WALLET_DOCTRINE.md`

Core domains

BRO keeps distinct owners for:

- market lifecycle and ownership truth
- lane permission and lane-gate truth
- wallet and capital authority
- edge measurement
- outcome interpretation

No lower layer may silently rename or reinterpret higher-layer truth into a
second dialect.

Timing authority law

Combat-locked sub-second synchronized timing is father-frame steel.
Combat-locked sub-second synchronized timing is required for honest maker/taker
readiness claims.

Authoritative timing truth requires:

- millisecond-class host clock synchronization
- explicit decision-to-submit evidence
- event/source-receive cross-domain skew visibility
- explicit separation between clock-health and latency-chain diagnosis

If timing truth is coarse, missing, degraded, or contradictory:

- BRO must fail closed, downgrade the claim, or halt
- coarse fallback timing must not certify combat readiness

Single semantic language law

One concept, one term.
One term, one meaning.
One decision boundary, one owner.

Forbidden:

- runtime/report parallel dialects
- legacy aliases surviving as live owners
- backfill/translation layers inside active runtime
- descriptive-only surfaces impersonating runtime authority

If a behavior problem is really a language problem, fix the highest-authority
semantic break first.

Ownership law

BRO may carry exactly one authoritative target pair at a time.

- one authoritative target is the runtime owner
- pending/prewarm candidates may exist for controlled warm-up
- pending/prewarm candidates have zero decision authority
- lifecycle watch truth must not impersonate active target-pair truth

Maker and taker are subordinate lanes inside the owned market, not separate
market lifecycles.

Lineage-stage law

`lineage_stage` is ancestry only.
It may be preserved for diagnostics, historical replay, and audit lineage.
It is not a live owner of market actionability.

Current lineage values may still include:

- `OBSERVE`
- `EVALUATE`
- `MAKER_POSITION`
- `MAKER_TAKER_SELECTIVE`
- `SNIPER_PRIMARY`
- `LATE_DIAGNOSTIC`
- `MAKER_LATE_WINDOW`
- `TAKER_COMMITMENT`
- `EXTREME_ONLY`
- `EXPIRED`
- `UNKNOWN`

Lineage-stage constraints:

- lineage may explain ancestry
- lineage may support historical replay
- lineage may not override lifecycle posture
- lineage may not recreate a second actionability owner

Canonical live taker authority

Taker authority is lifecycle-owned.
Canonical live taker authority lives in `taker_window` plus current taker
gates, not in old stage-family law.

Important historical boundary:

- `SNIPER_PRIMARY` is diagnostic-only in canonical live doctrine
- lineage-stage-local final-window overrides are reserved for explicit diagnostic or
  historical replay interpretation only
- `taker_decision` with conviction may still surface lineage for analysis, but
  the live submit path is owned by lifecycle and lane gates
- Canonical live taker authority is not active in this lineage stage.
