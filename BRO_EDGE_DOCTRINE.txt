Classification

- `current owner`
- measurement and execution-lane doctrine beneath `BRO_CANONICAL_DOCTRINE.txt`

Core rule

BRO has one edge model, one execution pathway, and one truth layer.
Edge truth measures reality; it does not create authority.

Timing authority law

Latency edge is only combat-real when timing truth is also real.
combat-locked sub-second synchronized timing is required for honest edge timing
claims.

Required timing truths:

- host clock discipline
- source/receive ordering truth
- decision-to-submit evidence

If these are weak, missing, degraded, or fallback-only:

- edge may be observable
- combat-grade maker/taker timing health is not proven

Lifecycle binding

Edge expression is bound to the lifecycle system:

- `scan`: maker no, taker no
- `prepare`: maker no, taker no
- `maker_window`: maker yes, taker no
- `taker_window`: maker no, taker yes
- `resolve`: maker no, taker no

Maker and taker are lifecycle-contained lanes, not separate stage machines.
