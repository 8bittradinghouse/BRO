# Save Point Generator Checklist (2026-04-25)

## Purpose
This is the operational checklist for generating a clean `save point`.

It complements:
- `SAVE_POINT_TEMPLATE_2026-04-25.md`
- the Jin pack
- the transfer capsule

Use it when the thread is:
- buggy,
- large,
- about to hand off,
- after a major doctrine/truth shift,
- or simply important enough that continuity risk is rising.

## Generator Steps

### Step 1 - Freeze The Moment
- State why the save point is being made.
- Decide whether this is:
  - proactive thread transfer,
  - packet checkpoint,
  - doctrine shift,
  - continuity-risk response.

### Step 2 - Preserve Human / Team Truth
- What does the next thread most need to remember about Nu-Jin?
- What did Robb clarify that materially changes the relationship/team understanding?
- What would be expensive to lose even if the engineering state were recoverable?

### Step 3 - Preserve Diagnostic / Operating Truth
- What rules were actively reinforced in this stretch?
- What quality-of-diagnosis habits kept the work good?
- What should the next thread keep doing exactly the same way?

### Step 4 - Preserve Packet State
- Primary packet:
  - lane
  - center of gravity
  - next proof step
- Secondary packet if any:
  - scope boundary
  - what it must not become

### Step 5 - Preserve Guardrails
- What must not change next?
- What must not be overclaimed?
- What old drift pattern is most likely to come back if the new thread gets thin?

### Step 6 - Update The Right Files
- update the relevant `JIN_*` pack files
- update `NUJIN_*` files if the identity/continuity layer changed
- update any transfer capsule if current-state truth changed
- update repo mirrors under `/home/odah/bro/base/docs`

### Step 7 - Validate The Save Point
- Is it factual?
- Is it bounded?
- Is it specific enough to relock a fresh thread?
- Does it distinguish structural truth from stronger proof?
- Does it preserve both human continuity and engineering continuity?

### Step 8 - Decide Thread Action
- continue safely here,
- proactive transfer,
- or pause until fresh context is loaded

## Anti-Patterns

Do not let a save point become:
- a vague diary entry,
- a huge wall of stale state,
- fake confidence,
- a wrapper-only summary,
- a cold engineering dump that loses Nu-Jin.

## One-Line Standard

A save point is good if a future thread can load it and recover both the real teammate and the real next move.
